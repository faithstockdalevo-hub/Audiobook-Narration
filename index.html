About Me

A voice you'll remember

Hello! I'm Faith Stockdale — an audiobook narrator with a passion for storytelling and a deep love of books. I believe every story deserves a voice that honors its characters, its world, and its emotional truth.

Whether it's a sweeping romance, a gripping thriller, or an inspiring non-fiction work, I bring warmth, clarity, and authentic characterization to every project I take on.

I work with publishers, production houses, and independent authors to deliver broadcast-quality recordings from my professional home studio.
